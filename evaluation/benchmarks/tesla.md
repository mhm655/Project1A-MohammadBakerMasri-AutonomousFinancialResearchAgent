# Tesla (TSLA) — Gold-standard summary

Gold-standard summary — drafted from SEC filings on 2026-10-01, pending human review.

## Business overview
Tesla designs, develops, manufactures, sells and leases high-performance fully electric vehicles and energy generation and storage systems. It has two reportable segments: automotive (vehicles, regulatory credits, services and other including used vehicles, Supercharging, insurance and parts) and energy generation and storage. The 10-K states the company is focused on bringing AI into the real world through Full Self-Driving (Supervised), Robotaxi and Optimus robots. Headquarters: Austin, TX; about 134,785 employees. (SEC EDGAR FY2025 10-K filed 2026-01-29, Item 1; employee count from the company profile tool)

## Key risks (FY2025 10-K Item 1A headings)
1. Issues or delays in developing, launching and ramping production of products and features.
2. Supplier failures and the need to secure battery cells, including through own cell manufacturing.
3. Demand for electric vehicles and adoption of autonomous driving; strong competition from established and new entrants.
4. Commercialising Bots such as Optimus in a nascent industry, and risks from international operations.

## Outlook points
- Gross margin was stable (18.0%) while operating income fell 38.5%: gross profit was nearly flat ($17.1 billion vs $17.5 billion), so operating expenses rose.
- Net income fell faster than operating income (-46.5% vs -38.5%).
- The stated strategy shifts weight towards AI, autonomy and robotics, which the risk factors describe as uncertain and dependent on production ramps.
