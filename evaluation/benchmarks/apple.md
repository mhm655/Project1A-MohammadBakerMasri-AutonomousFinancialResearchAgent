## Business overview
Apple designs, manufactures and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories, and sells related services. Product lines are iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home and Accessories; services include advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content and payments. Fiscal year ends on the last Saturday of September. Headquarters: Cupertino, CA; approximately 166,000 full-time equivalent employees at 27 September 2025. (SEC EDGAR FY2025 10-K filed 2025-10-31, Item 1)

## Key risks (FY2025 10-K Item 1A headings)
1. Global and regional economic conditions, and political events, trade disputes and geopolitical tensions.
2. Highly competitive markets with rapid technological change.
3. Dependence on outsourcing partners, many outside the U.S., and on obtaining components in sufficient quantity and on reasonable terms.
4. Frequent product and service transitions, and reliance on third-party intellectual property and software developers.

## Outlook points
- Growth accelerated in fiscal 2026: the June-quarter gain of 16.4% compares with 6.4% for fiscal 2025.
- Gross margin is expanding (50.1% in Q3 FY2026).
- Quarterly revenue is seasonal; the December quarter is the largest (Q1 FY2026 revenue $143.8 billion).
