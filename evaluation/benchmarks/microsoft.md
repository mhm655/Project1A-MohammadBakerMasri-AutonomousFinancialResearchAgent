# Microsoft (MSFT) — Gold-standard summary

Gold-standard summary — drafted from SEC filings on 2026-10-01, pending human review.

## Business overview
Microsoft develops and supports software, services, devices and solutions. It reports three segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. Its products span productivity and communication software, public, private and hybrid cloud and server products, operating systems, developer tools, gaming and devices; the 10-K frames its offerings around AI, cloud computing, productivity and collaboration, and personal computing. Headquarters: Redmond, WA; about 223,000 employees. (SEC EDGAR 10-K filed 2026-07-29, Item 1; employee count from the company profile tool)

## Key executives
Satya Nadella, Chairman and CEO; Amy E. Hood, Executive VP and CFO; Bradford L. Smith, President and Vice Chairman. (Company profile tool; officer names come from a market-data profile, not from the filing.)

## Key risks (10-K Item 1A headings)
1. Intense competition across all markets, including platform ecosystems and business-model competition.
2. The cloud and AI strategy requires substantial investment and depends on evolving customer demand, technology, competition and regulation.
3. Significant investments may not achieve expected returns; acquisitions and alliances may harm the business.
4. Cyberattacks and security vulnerabilities could reduce revenue, raise costs and damage reputation.

## Outlook points
- Profit grew faster than revenue in FY2026 (operating income +20.8% vs revenue +17.8%), so operating margin widened, while gross margin slipped 0.9 point.
- Net income and EPS grew about 31%, well ahead of operating income, so items below operating income contributed to the gain.
- The 10-K places AI and cloud at the centre of strategy and warns that this requires substantial investment; the return on that spending is the main swing factor.
