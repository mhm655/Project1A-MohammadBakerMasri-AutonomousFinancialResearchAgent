### Found, not yet fixed (for Day 13)
- A provider that answers `quota_exhausted`, or Ollama with a closed port, is still tried on every call. Each attempt adds latency: 31 rate-limit errors in the second C8 re-run.
- While no LLM is reachable, the review step keeps adding steps that need the LLM (6 calculation steps in the second C8 re-run). It should stop adding work when the provider chain is down.
- A continuation needs time left before the final deadline. In the second C8 re-run, rate-limit waits used up the research time, so the cut report could not be continued. Writing time should be reserved by elapsed wall-clock, not by plan progress.
