## Business Overview

Microsoft develops and sells a broad portfolio of technology solutions for individuals and enterprises.  Its business is organized into three segments:

| Segment | Core Offerings |
|---|---|
| **Productivity and Business Processes** | Microsoft 365 (Commercial & Consumer), LinkedIn, Dynamics 365, Power BI, and related security and compliance services[E5] |
| **Intelligent Cloud** | Azure cloud platform, server products (SQL Server, Windows Server), GitHub, AI and industry-specific cloud services, and enterprise support[E5] |
| **More Personal Computing** | Windows OS, Surface devices, Xbox gaming ecosystem, and related accessories[E5] |

Microsoft’s diversified revenue base and deep integration across enterprise and consumer ecosystems position it as a market leader in both cloud infrastructure and productivity software[E5].
