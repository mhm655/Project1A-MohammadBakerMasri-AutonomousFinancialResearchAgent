## Segment Performance

In Q3 FY2026 (period ended June 27, 2026), Apple achieved record June quarter performance across product and service divisions [E2, E7]. Products total revenue reached $78.7 billion, representing an 18% year-over-year increase [E2]. Within Products, Mac achieved its highest June quarter revenue on record at $10.4 billion, growing 29% year-over-year despite notable supply constraints [E7]. iPhone and Mac both posted double-digit growth rates to establish new June quarter records [E2]. Services revenue hit a June quarter record of $30.7 billion, up 12% year-over-year [E2, E7], supported by all-time revenue records in cloud services and payment services [E2]. Geographically, revenue grew by double digits across every geographic segment [E2, E4].
