# Microsoft: Company Profile

## Executive Summary

Microsoft Corporation (headquartered in Redmond, WA) is an enterprise and consumer technology provider operating across three main business segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing [E5]. Over the three-year period from FY2024 to FY2026, the company demonstrated top-line expansion, with revenue rising from $245.12 billion in FY2024 to $281.72 billion in FY2025 and $331.84 billion in FY2026 (ended 2026-06-30) [E6]. Operating margins expanded from 45.6% in FY2025 to 46.8% in FY2026, supported by strong revenue growth of 17.8% in FY2026 [E6, E7, E8, E9]. Among infrastructure software peers, Microsoft ranks first in total revenue and operating margin, while ranking fourth in revenue growth [E7]. Looking ahead, if Microsoft maintains its cloud and AI monetization trajectory, revenue growth may remain durable, though investors should monitor executive transitions and ongoing product rethinks as key operational factors; no formal management guidance figures were provided in the evidence [E4].

## Business Overview

Microsoft Corporation operates globally across three core business segments [E5]:
- **Productivity and Business Processes:** Includes Microsoft 365 Commercial and Consumer offerings, Enterprise Mobility + Security, Power BI, Exchange, SharePoint, Teams, Microsoft 365 Copilot, LinkedIn, and Dynamics 365 cloud and on-premises applications [E5].
- **Intelligent Cloud:** Comprises server products and cloud services, including Azure consumption-based services, GitHub, SQL Server, Windows Server, Visual Studio, and enterprise support services [E5].
- **More Personal Computing:** Covers Windows operating systems, devices (PCs, tablets, accessories), video games, and gaming consoles [E5].

As of FY2026, the company employs approximately 223,000 people and maintains a market capitalization of $3.81 trillion [E5]. In a peer comparison against major software and security vendors (Palantir, Oracle, Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, and Fortinet), Microsoft leads the group in total revenue ($331.84 billion) and operating margin (46.8%), while ranking fourth in revenue growth rate (17.8%) [E7].

Analysis: Microsoft's scale and leading operating margin (46.8%) provide substantial cash generation to fund massive cloud and AI infrastructure investments relative to smaller niche peers like Palantir (56.2% revenue growth) or CrowdStrike [E7].
