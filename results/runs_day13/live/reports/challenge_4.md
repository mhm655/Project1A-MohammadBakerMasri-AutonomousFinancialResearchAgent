**Analysis:** Microsoft’s margin expansion likely stems from a favorable product mix (higher-margin Azure services and AI offerings) and continued cost discipline, as noted in its 10-K risk discussion of “substantial investments” and “cost control” [E10].  Alphabet’s flat margin suggests pricing pressure from competitive cloud pricing and rising R&D costs, while Amazon’s low margin reflects aggressive expansion of data-center capacity and fulfillment infrastructure.

## Conclusion

Microsoft’s Azure division is delivering the fastest revenue growth (≈ 18 % FY2026) and the highest operating margin (≈ 47 %) among the three cloud leaders, suggesting a durable profitability advantage if AI-driven demand persists [E17].  Alphabet shows solid growth and stable margins, while Amazon’s AWS, despite being the largest in absolute revenue, operates at a much lower margin, indicating ongoing heavy investment.  **Analysis:** The outlook hinges on each firm’s ability to translate AI and hybrid-cloud opportunities into higher-margin revenue; absent explicit cloud-segment data, future assessments should monitor disclosed segment results, guidance in upcoming 10-Ks, and sentiment trends for signs of competitive shifts.
