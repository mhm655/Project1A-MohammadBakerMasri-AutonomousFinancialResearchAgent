## Company Overview

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) operates as a data center scale AI infrastructure company in the United States, Taiwan, China, Hong Kong, Europe, and internationally [E5]. The company is incorporated in 1993 and is headquartered in Santa Clara, California, with approximately 42,000 employees [E5]. It operates through two primary segments: Compute & Networking and Graphics [E5]. The Compute & Networking segment provides data center accelerated computing, networking platforms, AI solutions, and automotive platforms [E5]. The Graphics segment offers GeForce GPUs for gaming and PCs, as well as Quadro/NVIDIA RTX GPUs for enterprise workstation graphics [E5]. NVIDIA sells its products to original equipment manufacturers, system integrators, cloud service providers, and automotive manufacturers [E5]. The company is led by Co-Founder and CEO Jen-Hsun Huang, with Colette M. Kress serving as Executive VP & CFO [E5].
