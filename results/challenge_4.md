# MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL: Comparative Analysis

## Executive Summary

The cloud computing market is dominated by three hyperscalers: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud (GCP). In the most recent fiscal periods, Microsoft reported FY2026 (ended 2026-06-30) revenue of $331.8 billion with a 46.8% operating margin [E11], while Alphabet reported FY2025 (ended 2025-12-31) revenue of $402.8 billion with a 32.0% operating margin [E11]. Amazon reported FY2025 (ended 2025-12-31) revenue of $716.9 billion with an 11.2% operating margin [E11]. Google Cloud demonstrated the strongest recent growth acceleration, with Q2 FY2026 revenue increasing 82% to $24.8 billion [E8]. Analysis: While AWS maintains the largest market share, Microsoft Azure leads in profitability, and Google Cloud is currently the fastest-growing segment among the three.
