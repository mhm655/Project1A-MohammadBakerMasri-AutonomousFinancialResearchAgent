# MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL: Comparative Analysis

## Executive Summary

Amazon (AMZN), Microsoft (MSFT), and Alphabet (GOOGL) dominate the cloud infrastructure market, with Amazon holding the largest revenue base at $716.92 billion for FY2025 [E16]. Microsoft demonstrates the highest profitability with a 46.8% operating margin for FY2026 [E16], while Alphabet reports a 32.0% operating margin for FY2025 [E16]. Amazon's revenue growth of 12.38% in FY2025 trails both Microsoft's 17.79% in FY2026 and Alphabet's 15.09% in FY2025 [E16]. Analysis: The competitive landscape is defined by Amazon's scale and market share leadership, Microsoft's superior margin profile, and Alphabet's recent acceleration in cloud revenue growth.

## Margins

Operating margins vary significantly across the three companies, reflecting their different business mixes. ## Competitive Advantages

Each company cites intense competition as a primary risk factor in their respective 10-K filings [E10, E12, E13]. Microsoft specifically notes that its cloud and AI strategy requires substantial investments and depends on evolving customer demand and technological developments [E13]. Amazon highlights risks related to optimizing and operating its fulfillment network and data centers, as well as the strain of expansion on management and operational resources [E10]. Alphabet points to the inherent risks of increasing investment in new businesses and the need to continue innovating to maintain its competitive position [E12].

Analysis: The competitive landscape is defined by massive capital expenditure and the race to integrate AI into cloud services. Microsoft's advantage appears to be its high-margin enterprise software ecosystem, while Amazon's strength lies in its integrated retail and logistics network supporting AWS. Alphabet's advantage is its dominant search and advertising base, which funds its cloud and AI initiatives.

## Conclusion

The cloud computing divisions of Amazon, Microsoft, and Alphabet are all growing, but they differ significantly in scale, profitability, and growth rate. Microsoft leads in operating margin (46.8% in FY2026) and revenue growth (17.79% in FY2026), while Amazon has the largest total revenue ($716.92 billion in FY2025) but the lowest operating margin (11.2%) and slowest revenue growth (12.38%) among the three [E16]. Alphabet shows strong momentum, with Google Cloud revenues increasing 82% to $24.8 billion in Q2 FY2026 [E7], and consolidated operating margin expanding to 34% in Q2 FY2026 [E17].

Analysis: The competitive dynamic is shifting from pure infrastructure scale to AI-driven value creation. Microsoft's high margins and strong growth suggest it is currently capturing the most value from its cloud investments. Amazon's lower margins reflect its broader, more capital-intensive business model. Alphabet's rapid cloud acceleration indicates it is closing the gap with its peers. Investors should watch for continued margin expansion at Alphabet and whether Amazon can improve its operating leverage as its cloud business matures.
