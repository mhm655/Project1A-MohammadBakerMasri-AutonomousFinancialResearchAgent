## Executive Summary

Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, Citigroup, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are the major U.S. diversified banks covered.  Across FY2023-FY2025, revenue grew for all except Wells Fargo (FY2019 data) and margins showed mixed trends: Bank of America and Goldman Sachs improved margins, while JPMorgan Chase and Citigroup saw margin pressure.  The strongest revenue growth came from Goldman Sachs (+8.9% FY2025) and Bank of America (+6.8% FY2025), whereas JPMorgan Chase’s growth slowed to +2.8% FY2025 and its net margin fell 1.7 pp. *(unverified: no source cited)*  Overall, profitability remains high for the largest peers, but margin dynamics reflect differing pricing, mix and cost-control outcomes.

- **Goldman Sachs Group Inc. (GS)** – Provides investment banking, securities, and asset & wealth management through Global Banking & Markets, Asset & Wealth Management and Platform Solutions [E11].
