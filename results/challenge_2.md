CFO Kevan Parekh noted that foreign exchange is expected to drive about a 5 percentage point headwind to the year-over-year growth rate from the March to September quarter [E5]. Analysis: The emphasis on domestic manufacturing partnerships and the specific quantification of FX headwinds suggest management is proactively addressing supply chain localization and currency risks in their communication strategy.

## Guidance and Outlook

For the fiscal fourth quarter of 2026 (the September quarter), Apple provided the following guidance [E5]:
*   Total company revenue growth: 9%-11% year-over-year
*   Gross margin: 47%-48%
*   Operating expenses: $19.1 billion-$19.4 billion
*   Other income (expense), net: Around $350 million (excluding mark-to-market of minority investments)
*   Tax rate: Around 16.5%

Analysis: The guidance implies a sequential revenue decline from the fiscal third quarter 2026 revenue of $109.4 billion [E4], consistent with Apple's typical seasonal pattern where the September quarter is the peak holiday quarter but the June quarter is also strong. The gross margin guidance of 47%-48% is lower than the 50.06% gross margin achieved in the fiscal third quarter 2026 [E4, E6], suggesting potential margin pressure or mix shifts in the upcoming quarter. *(unverified)*
